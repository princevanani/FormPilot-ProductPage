# Privacy Policy

Plain-language summary: everything runs on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

## 1. Overview

FormPilot ("the extension," "we," "our") is a Chrome browser extension that helps you copy data from an image or PDF document and fill matching fields into a web form. This policy explains what data the extension handles, where it is processed, and what is — and is not — stored. It applies to the FormPilot browser extension and to this website.

## 2. What FormPilot does

- Reads a document (image or PDF) you choose to upload, or a screenshot you choose to capture, and extracts text from it using on-device optical character recognition (OCR) and PDF parsing.
- Scans the form fields present on a web page — only when you click **"Scan this form."**
- Fills form fields on a web page — only when you click **"Fill form now."**
- Lets you right-click an image or a page to start an extraction directly, as a shortcut to the same upload flow.

## 3. Where your data is processed

All document processing — OCR, PDF text extraction, and field matching — happens locally, inside your own browser, on your own device. Files you upload and screenshots you capture are **never uploaded** to any server operated by us or by anyone else. There is no backend server that FormPilot's core functionality depends on.

The one exception: the first time you use OCR in a given language (for example, Hindi or Gujarati), the extension downloads that language's recognition data from a public, third-party host, `tessdata.projectnaptha.com`. This is a one-time download of a generic language model — it contains no information about you, your documents, or your activity, and nothing about your request is personally identifying.

## 4. What is stored, and where

FormPilot stores two kinds of information, both locally on your device via Chrome's built-in `chrome.storage.local` API:

- **Confirmed field mappings** — for example, "on this website, 'Customer Name' means 'Party Name'" — saved per website domain, so you don't have to re-confirm familiar matches the next time you use that site.
- **Your preferences** — your chosen interface language and OCR language selection.

None of this is transmitted to us or to any third party at any point. It stays on your device, inside your browser profile, and is only accessible to the FormPilot extension itself. You can clear it at any time via **"Forget remembered fields for this site"** in the extension's settings panel, or by removing the extension entirely, which deletes all locally stored data along with it.

## 5. What FormPilot does not do

- Does not require an account, sign-in, or any personal information to function.
- Does not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties, under any circumstance.
- Does not use your data for advertising, profiling, or cross-site tracking.
- Does not transmit the content of any document, form, or web page to any server.
- Does not run in the background monitoring your browsing — it only acts when you click Scan or Fill.

## 6. Permissions FormPilot requests

Chrome requires an extension to declare upfront every capability it may use. Here is what each permission FormPilot requests is for, in plain terms:

- `storage` — to save field-mapping preferences and settings locally, as described in section 4.
- `scripting`, `activeTab`, `tabs` — to scan and fill the form in the tab you select, and to list your open tabs so you can choose one.
- `contextMenus` — to add the right-click options "Extract data from this image" and "Extract data from this page."
- `sidePanel` — to display the extension's interface.
- **Access to all websites** — because the source document and the destination form can be on any website you choose. FormPilot only reads or modifies a page's content when you explicitly click Scan or Fill.

## 7. Third-party services

FormPilot's OCR engine (Tesseract.js) and PDF engine (pdf.js) are open-source libraries that run entirely inside the extension, on your device — they are not third-party services that receive your data. The single external request FormPilot makes is the one-time OCR language-data download described in section 3. This website itself does not use third-party analytics or advertising trackers.

## 8. Your choices and controls

- You decide which documents to upload and which forms to fill — nothing happens automatically.
- You can review and edit every extracted field and every suggested match before anything is filled.
- You can clear remembered mappings for any one website, at any time.
- You can uninstall the extension at any time via `chrome://extensions`, which removes all locally stored data.

## 9. Children's privacy

FormPilot is a productivity tool intended for general, professional use (for example, by accountants and data-entry operators) and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect data from children, and given it collects no personal data from anyone, there is no age-gated functionality to speak of.

## 10. International users

Because FormPilot does not transmit or store your data on any server, there is no cross-border data transfer to disclose: your information never leaves your own device, regardless of where you are located.

## 11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a revised "Last updated" date above. Material changes will also be reflected in the extension's Chrome Web Store listing.

# Frequently asked questions

Can't find what you need? Get in touch.

General

Is FormPilot free?

Yes. Every feature currently available is free, with no account, trial limit, or credit card required. We may introduce an optional paid tier for teams later, but the features that are free today are intended to stay free.

Do I need to create an account?

No. FormPilot works entirely without sign-in. There's nothing to register for and nothing stored about you beyond the on-device preferences described in the Privacy Policy.

What browsers does FormPilot support?

FormPilot is built for Google Chrome (Manifest V3) and other Chromium-based browsers that support the same extension platform, such as Microsoft Edge and Brave.

Will there be a paid version?

Possibly, for team-oriented features like shared mappings or usage history — see the Pricing section on the home page. Nothing free today will be taken away or put behind a paywall.

Using FormPilot

What file types can I upload?

Images (JPG, PNG, and similar formats) and PDF files — both scanned/image-based PDFs and PDFs with a native text layer or fillable form fields.

Which languages are supported?

Field matching and the extension's own interface currently support English, Hindi, and Gujarati. More languages can be added to the matching dictionary over time.

Does FormPilot work on every website?

FormPilot can scan and fill standard form fields — text inputs, dropdowns, checkboxes, radio buttons, and editable text areas — on most websites. Some heavily customized or non-standard form widgets may not be recognized.

What happens if a field isn't matched?

FormPilot never guesses silently. Any extracted field it can't confidently match to a destination field is clearly flagged as unmatched, so you can fill it manually or adjust the match yourself.

Can I correct a match before filling?

Yes. Every suggested match is shown with a confidence score before anything is filled, and you can review or override it.

Can I use FormPilot on a PDF opened directly in Chrome's built-in viewer?

FormPilot works best when you upload the PDF file directly into the side panel. Reading a PDF that's merely open in a browser tab (rather than uploaded) depends on how that page serves the file.

Privacy & security

Is my data safe?

Yes. All processing — OCR, PDF parsing, and field matching — happens locally in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to any server. See the full Privacy Policy for details.

Does FormPilot store my documents?

No. Documents are processed in memory while you work with them and are not retained afterward. The only thing FormPilot stores is a small record of which field labels you've confirmed match on a given website — not the documents or data themselves.

Why does FormPilot need access to all websites?

Because the source document and destination form can be on any two unrelated websites you choose. FormPilot only reads or modifies a page when you explicitly click Scan or Fill — it does not run in the background.

Troubleshooting & support

Why didn't a field get filled correctly?

Most often this means OCR misread a character, or the field's label was unusual enough that it fell below the matching confidence threshold. Review the extracted text and the suggested match before filling — you can always correct either one.

How do I report a bug or request a feature?

Use the Contact page, or reach out at the support email listed there. Specific examples (a screenshot, the website involved) help a lot.

Is FormPilot open source?

FormPilot's OCR and PDF-parsing components are built on the open-source Tesseract.js and pdf.js projects. The extension itself is not currently open source.
