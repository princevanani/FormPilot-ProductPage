Features

Everything a data-entry day needs

Built specifically around the busywork of accountants, operators, and anyone who copies data for a living.

Smart synonym matching

Recognizes that "GST", "GSTIN", "GST No." and "GST Number" are the same field — and that "Party Name" and "Customer Name" are too.

OCR + PDF extraction

Reads scanned images and PDFs alike, including fillable PDF forms, without manual retyping.

Multi-language support

Field matching and the interface work in English, Hindi, and Gujarati — with more languages supported.

Works on any website

The source document and the destination form can be on two completely different tabs or windows — your choice, every time.

Remembers your choices

Confirm a match once on a given website, and FormPilot recalls it automatically next time you fill that domain.

Private by design

Document text is processed locally in your browser. Document files are never uploaded to any cloud server.

Knows a reworded label when it sees one.

  • Confidence score shown for every suggested match

FAQ

Quick answers

How does the 30-day free trial work?
When you open the FormPilot extension side panel for the first time, simply enter your email address. We send a 6-digit verification code to confirm your email. Once entered, your 30-day trial activates with full, unlimited access. No credit card required!
Is my document data safe?
Yes — document text recognition and field matching run locally inside your browser. FormPilot does not upload your invoices, screenshots, or forms to any server. Check our full Privacy Policy for complete details.
What languages are supported?
Field matching and the extension's user interface support English, Hindi, and Gujarati out of the box.

30-Day Free Trial
How do I start the free trial?
Install the extension and open the side panel. Enter your email address to receive a 6-digit OTP code. Enter the code and your 30-day trial begins immediately. Everyone is eligible!
Is a credit card required?
No! No credit card or payment information is required to activate and enjoy the 30-day free trial.
Using FormPilot
What file types can I upload?
Images (JPG, PNG, WEBP) and PDF files — both scanned image PDFs and native text PDFs with forms and tables.
Does it work across different tabs?
Yes! You can have your invoice or document open in one tab, and FormPilot will scan and autofill forms open in any other tab or browser window.
Privacy & Security
Does FormPilot send my invoices to a server?
No. All OCR and document parsing runs locally on your computer inside the browser extension. Your document data is never sent to our servers. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
